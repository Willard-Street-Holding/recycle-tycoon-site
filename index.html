REcycle Tycoon

Build, Upgrade, and Automate Your Recycling Empire

About the Game

REcycle Tycoon is a casual 3D arcade management game where players collect raw recyclables, run automated assembly lines, upgrade processing machinery, and expand their factory operations.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how REcycle Tycoon ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require users to create an account or provide personal identifying information (such as your real name, address, or phone number) to play REcycle Tycoon.

  • Device & Gameplay Data: We may collect non-personal device identifiers, operating system details, and in-game performance metrics to fix bugs and optimize performance.
  • Ad Networks & Monetization: The game utilizes third-party advertising SDKs (such as Unity Ads) to show rewarded video ads. These services may collect anonymized device identifiers (IP address, advertising ID) to serve relevant ads.

2. How Information is Used

Collected data is strictly used to maintain game functionality, serve optional rewarded advertisements, and improve user retention and performance.

3. Third-Party Services

REcycle Tycoon uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify your device:

  • Unity Services & Unity Ads Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Services

4. Children's Privacy

Our game is designed for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13. If you believe personal data from a child has been collected, please contact us so we can take immediate action.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

All gameplay progress is stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored game data. For inquiries regarding any third-party ad data, please contact us via support email.

6. Updates to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
