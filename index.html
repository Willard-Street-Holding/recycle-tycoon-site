About Willard Street Holdings

Willard Street Holdings, LLC is a private holding and asset management firm headquartered in Redlands, California. The company acquires, develops, and manages a diversified portfolio of core assets, spanning digital intellectual property, mobile application development, software publishing, and strategic real estate investments across major regional markets including Pennsylvania and Virginia.

Holdings & Operating Divisions

Digital Media & Mobile Publishing

Engineering and operating mobile software products, interactive games, and enterprise tools across iOS and Android platforms. Lead digital assets include REcycle TYcoon, a landscape hybrid-casual simulation game built in Unity.

Platform: iOS & Android

Intellectual Property & Licensing

Holding, structuring, and licensing proprietary digital assets, media tools, corporate brand marks, and specialized workflow technology platforms.

Focus: IP Management & Brand Strategy

Real Estate Investments

Acquiring, rehabilitating, and actively managing residential rental properties and real estate holdings with a focus on long-term portfolio growth and liquidity management.

Markets: Philadelphia, PA & Virginia
